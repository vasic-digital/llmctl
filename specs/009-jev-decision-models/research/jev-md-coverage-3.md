## 3. Contradictions inside this slice

1. **llama.cpp support**: 3682/3699-3701 say llama.cpp is unsuitable and unsupported for these models; 3691 and 3711 say Laya and Kev run under llama.cpp (GGUF backend of jev-style / jev-compatible-server); 3715 says "don't standardize on it".
2. **Ecosystem direction**: 3701 says the tools "prioritize ONNX/Core ML/PyTorch over GGUF" while 3711 says jev-style and jev-compatible-server have a GGUF backend.
3. **"Only path to fine-tune"**: 3010/3013/3344 call Kev the only fine-tunable model; 2671 (Verdict distill), 2672 (Laya fine-tune bases), 2725 (Strands Decider "fine-tunable, open training recipe") say otherwise.
4. **Automation claims**: 3384 "cannot happen without any manual involvement" vs 3678 "only manual steps are defining the task and spot-checking" vs the 3667-3676 table marking domain fine-tuning (needs data prep and a GPU) as "Automatic".
